# Governance

rappidAI Quantum is currently maintained by Jonas Désiré Cikemgil as the
primary and sole maintainer. No foundation, company team, steering committee,
or formal voting body exists.

The maintainer decides roadmap, releases, security handling, licensing, and
merge acceptance. Material decisions should be documented in issues or pull
requests with their evidence and trade-offs. Contributors may propose changes,
but contribution does not grant commit access or governance authority.

If maintainership expands, this document should define roles, nomination and
removal, decision rules, conflict handling, and succession before implying
shared governance.
