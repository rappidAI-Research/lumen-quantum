# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Separate evidence, hypotheses, and opinions.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when appropriate.
- Protect private, sensitive, and security-relevant information.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, and
sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `cikemgil@rappidai-research.com`. The sole
maintainer will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality where possible,
and may edit, remove, warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban behavior.
Conflicts involving the maintainer have no independent escalation body today;
that governance limitation is documented rather than concealed.

This policy is based on Contributor Covenant 2.1.
